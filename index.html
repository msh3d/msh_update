1 ct = 0.2 g · 1 ozt = 31.1035 g · 1 dwt = 1.55517 g · 1 tola = 11.6638 g · 1 in = 25.4 mm. Weight units convert to weight units, length to length.

Conversion table used

Everything is derived from the ISO 8653 inner circumference (C). US: inner diameter = 11.63 + 0.8128 × size, C = π × diameter. EU = C in mm. UK: C = 37.5 + 1.25 × letter steps (A = 0, half sizes allowed; type N, N½ or Z+1). Japan: size = 3C/π − 38. India: size = C − 40, an approximation, so confirm with your vendor. Sizes are rounded for display.

Alloy density varies by recipe (copper, silver, zinc and palladium content), so 22K, 18K and 14K values are approximations. Edit the density to match your alloy. Wax mode: metal weight = wax weight × metal density ÷ wax density. Casting wax is roughly 0.9–1.0 g/cc; castable resins are often 1.1–1.2.

Metal cost uses weight plus wastage. Per-gram making charge is applied to the net weight. Markup is applied to the subtotal and GST to the marked-up value. If your making charges are taxed at a different rate than metal, adjust the GST % to suit. In the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" to export.
